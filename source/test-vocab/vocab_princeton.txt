#PART 1: Options from Words in Context Questions (Module 1 Q1–7 & Module 2 Q1–6)
hazardous|adj.|dangerous; likely to cause harm|危險的|Workers must wear masks when handling ______ chemicals.|處理「危險的」化學物質時，工人必須戴口罩。
active|adj.|(of a volcano) able to erupt; busy doing things|活躍的；活的（火山）|Scientists watch the ______ volcano closely for signs of an eruption.|科學家密切觀察這座「活」火山是否有噴發跡象。
mountainous|adj.|having many mountains|多山的|Travel is slow in ______ regions where roads twist around steep peaks.|在道路繞著陡峭山峰的「多山」地區，交通很緩慢。
remote|adj.|far away from towns and people|偏遠的|The village is so ______ that the mail arrives only once a week.|那個村莊非常「偏遠」，郵件一週只送來一次。
traditional|adj.|following customs that have existed for a long time|傳統的|My grandmother still cooks ______ dishes for the Lunar New Year.|我的祖母過農曆新年時仍然煮「傳統的」菜餚。
obscure|adj.|not well known|鮮為人知的；冷門的|The author's first novel remained ______ until a famous director turned it into a film.|這位作家的第一部小說一直「鮮為人知」，直到一位知名導演把它拍成電影。
discreet|adj.|careful not to attract attention|謹慎低調的|Please be ______ and don't tell anyone about the surprise party.|請「低調」一點，不要告訴任何人這場驚喜派對。
eccentric|adj.|strange or unusual in behavior|古怪的|The ______ artist painted only while standing on one foot.|那位「古怪的」藝術家只在單腳站立時作畫。
formal|adj.|following official rules and customs|正式的|Guests at the ______ dinner were asked to wear suits and gowns.|參加「正式」晚宴的賓客被要求穿西裝和禮服。
bustling|adj.|full of busy activity and people|熱鬧的；繁忙的|The ______ night market was crowded with shoppers and food stalls.|「熱鬧的」夜市擠滿了購物人潮和小吃攤。
unpredictable|adj.|not able to be known in advance|難以預測的|Mountain weather is ______, so always bring a raincoat.|山上的天氣「難以預測」，所以一定要帶雨衣。
deserted|adj.|empty; with no people|荒涼的；無人的|After midnight, the train station was completely ______.|午夜過後，火車站完全「空無一人」。
characteristic|n.|a typical quality or feature|特徵；特性|Long ears are a key ______ of this rabbit breed.|長耳朵是這種兔子品種的主要「特徵」。
prospect|n.|the chance of future success|前景；展望|Learning English can improve your job ______s.|學英文能改善你的工作「前景」。
efficacy|n.|the ability to produce the desired result|功效；效力|Doctors tested the ______ of the new medicine on 500 patients.|醫生在 500 名病人身上測試新藥的「功效」。
repercussion|n.|a bad effect or result of an action|不良後果；影響|Cheating on the exam had serious ______s for his future.|考試作弊對他的未來造成了嚴重的「後果」。
aggregate|v.|to collect or gather into one group or amount|彙集；聚集|The app ______s news from many websites into one page.|這個應用程式把許多網站的新聞「彙集」到同一頁。
dissipate|v.|to gradually disappear or spread out|消散|The morning fog began to ______ as the sun rose.|太陽升起時，晨霧開始「消散」。
percolate|v.|(of liquid) to pass slowly through something|滲透；過濾|Rainwater ______s through the soil and reaches underground streams.|雨水「滲透」過土壤，流入地下水流。
collaborate|v.|to work together with others|合作|Students ______d on a science project about clean water.|學生們「合作」完成一個關於乾淨用水的科學專題。
corroborate|v.|to support or confirm with evidence|證實；佐證|Two witnesses ______d the driver's story.|兩名目擊者「證實」了司機的說法。
exonerate|v.|to officially show that someone is not guilty|證明……無罪|New evidence helped ______ the man who had been wrongly accused.|新證據幫助「證明」那名被誤控的男子「無罪」。
contextualize|v.|to explain something by describing its background situation|把……置於背景脈絡中理解|The museum guide ______d the painting by describing life in 1800s Paris.|博物館導覽員描述 1800 年代巴黎的生活，把這幅畫「置於背景脈絡中」說明。
postulate|v.|to suggest something as true as a basis for reasoning|假定；提出假設|Scientists ______ that the planet may have water beneath its ice.|科學家「假定」這顆行星的冰層下可能有水。
characterize|v.|to describe the typical qualities of|描述……的特徵；形容為|Critics ______ her music as calm and hopeful.|評論家把她的音樂「形容為」平靜而充滿希望。
evince|v.|to clearly show a feeling or quality|表現出；顯示出|His careful notes ______d a deep interest in the subject.|他仔細的筆記「顯示出」他對這個科目的濃厚興趣。
elide|v.|to leave out or omit|省略|The summary ______d many small details to stay short.|這份摘要「省略」了許多小細節以保持簡短。
gauge|v.|to measure or judge something|測量；判斷|Teachers use quizzes to ______ how much students have learned.|老師用小考來「判斷」學生學了多少。
collaborate on|phr.|to work together on something|在……上合作|Two companies will ______ a new electric car.|兩家公司將「合作」開發一款新電動車。
invest with|phr.|to give official power or authority to|授予（權力）|Judges are ______ the power to interpret the law.|法官「被授予」解釋法律的權力。|invested with
plot against|phr.|to secretly plan to harm someone|密謀反對|In the play, the king's brother ______ him.|在劇中，國王的弟弟「密謀反對」他。|plots against
learn from|phr.|to gain knowledge or skill by studying something|向……學習|Good players ______ their mistakes.|好的球員會從錯誤中「學習」。
intention|n.|something you plan to do|意圖；打算|She has no ______ of quitting the team.|她沒有退出球隊的「打算」。
agenda|n.|a list of things to be discussed at a meeting|議程|The first item on the ______ is the school trip.|「議程」上的第一項是校外教學。
distraction|n.|something that takes your attention away|使人分心的事物|Music can be a ______ when you are studying math.|讀數學時，音樂可能是一種「干擾」。
technique|n.|a particular way of doing something|技巧；方法|The coach showed us a new breathing ______ for swimming.|教練教我們一種新的游泳換氣「技巧」。
transmission|n.|the act of passing something from one to another|傳遞；傳播|Washing hands reduces the ______ of germs.|洗手能減少細菌的「傳播」。
confidence|n.|a feeling of being sure of yourself|信心；自信|Practicing every day gave him the ______ to speak in public.|每天練習給了他公開演講的「信心」。
prolongation|n.|the act of making something last longer|延長|The ______ of the summer vacation made students happy.|暑假的「延長」讓學生很開心。
devastation|n.|great damage or destruction|嚴重破壞；浩劫|The earthquake left ______ across the city.|地震在全市留下了「嚴重的破壞」。
disproportionately|adv.|to a much greater degree than others|不成比例地；過度地|Air pollution ______ affects children and the elderly.|空氣污染「不成比例地」影響兒童和老人。
exclusively|adv.|only; not including anything else|唯一地；專門地|This parking lot is ______ for teachers.|這個停車場「專門」給老師使用。
essentially|adv.|basically; in the most important ways|本質上；基本上|The two phones are ______ the same, except for color.|這兩支手機除了顏色以外「基本上」一樣。
initially|adv.|at first; at the beginning|起初；最初|______, I didn't like spicy food, but now I love it.|「起初」我不喜歡辣的食物，但現在很愛。
trivialize|v.|to make something seem less important than it is|輕視；使顯得微不足道|Don't ______ your friend's worries by laughing at them.|不要嘲笑朋友的煩惱而「輕視」它們。
illustrate|v.|to explain or show with pictures or examples|說明；以圖解釋|The teacher used a map to ______ the route of the explorers.|老師用地圖來「說明」探險家的路線。
dismiss|v.|to reject an idea as not worth considering|駁斥；不予理會|Scientists ______ed the rumor as false.|科學家「駁斥」這個謠言是假的。
promote|v.|to encourage or support something|推廣；促進|The campaign aims to ______ healthy eating in schools.|這項活動旨在「推廣」校園健康飲食。
distribute|v.|to give out to many people|分發；分配|Volunteers ______d food to families after the storm.|暴風雨過後，志工「分發」食物給家庭。
emphasize|v.|to make something more noticeable or important|強調；突顯|The speaker ______d the importance of sleep.|演講者「強調」睡眠的重要性。
#PART 2: Key Words from Module 1 Passages
intergenerational|adj.|involving people of different ages or generations|跨世代的|The ______ choir has singers from age 8 to 80.|這個「跨世代」合唱團的團員從 8 歲到 80 歲都有。
intangible|adj.|not physical; unable to be touched|無形的；非物質的|Friendship is an ______ treasure.|友誼是一種「無形的」寶藏。
throng|n.|a large crowd of people|人群；群眾|______s of fans waited outside the stadium.|「大批」粉絲在體育場外等候。
obstacle|n.|something that blocks progress|障礙|Lack of money was the biggest ______ to his dream.|缺錢是他實現夢想最大的「障礙」。
incidence|n.|how often something happens|發生率|The ______ of flu rises in winter.|流感的「發生率」在冬天上升。
patent|n.|an official right to be the only one to make or sell an invention|專利|She got a ______ for her new kind of bicycle lock.|她為她的新型腳踏車鎖取得了「專利」。
reservoir|n.|a large natural or artificial lake used to store water|水庫；蓄水池|The city gets its drinking water from a ______ in the hills.|這座城市的飲用水來自山上的「水庫」。
compelling|adj.|very convincing or interesting|令人信服的；引人入勝的|The lawyer presented ______ evidence.|律師提出了「令人信服的」證據。
inclusive|adj.|including and welcoming all kinds of people|包容的|Our club tries to be ______ of students from every background.|我們的社團努力「包容」各種背景的學生。
invariably|adv.|always; without exception|總是；必然|He ______ arrives five minutes early.|他「總是」提早五分鐘到。
preternaturally|adv.|beyond what is normal or natural|超乎尋常地；異常地|The puppy was ______ calm during the thunderstorm.|小狗在雷雨中「異常地」冷靜。
anthropogenic|adj.|caused by human activity|人為的；人類造成的|______ pollution has harmed many rivers.|「人為」污染已傷害了許多河流。
refuse|n.|waste material; garbage|垃圾；廢物|Seagulls search the harbor for ______ from fishing boats.|海鷗在港口尋找漁船丟棄的「廢物」。
proximity|n.|nearness in space or time|接近；鄰近|The hotel's ______ to the beach makes it popular.|這家飯店「靠近」海灘，所以很受歡迎。
proponent|n.|a person who supports an idea|支持者；擁護者|She is a strong ______ of free public libraries.|她是免費公共圖書館的堅定「支持者」。
controversial|adj.|causing a lot of disagreement|有爭議的|The new rule about phones was ______ among students.|關於手機的新規定在學生之間「頗具爭議」。
celestial|adj.|related to the sky or outer space|天體的；天空的|Stars and planets are ______ bodies.|恆星和行星都是「天體」。
inflammation|n.|a red, swollen, painful condition in part of the body|發炎|Ice can reduce ______ after a sports injury.|冰敷可以減輕運動傷害後的「發炎」。
amputation|n.|the surgical removal of a body part|截肢|After the accident, doctors had to perform an ______ of his leg.|意外發生後，醫生不得不為他的腿進行「截肢」。
dilemma|n.|a difficult choice between two options|兩難；困境|She faced a ______: study for the test or go to her friend's party.|她面臨「兩難」：準備考試還是去朋友的派對。
irrational|adj.|not based on reason or logic|不理性的|His fear of butterflies is ______.|他對蝴蝶的恐懼是「不理性的」。
pilgrimage|n.|a journey to a holy place|朝聖之旅|Every year, millions make a ______ to the temple.|每年有數百萬人到這座寺廟「朝聖」。
avant-garde|adj.|new and experimental, especially in art|前衛的|The ______ concert used sounds from kitchen pots.|這場「前衛」音樂會使用了廚房鍋具的聲音。
eradicate|v.|to completely destroy or get rid of|根除；消滅|Vaccines helped ______ smallpox.|疫苗幫助「根除」了天花。
trilogy|n.|a set of three related books or films|三部曲|I finished the whole fantasy ______ in one week.|我一週內就讀完了整套奇幻「三部曲」。
unclaimed|adj.|not owned or demanded by anyone|無人認領的；無人宣稱主權的|The lost-and-found box is full of ______ umbrellas.|失物招領箱裡滿是「無人認領的」雨傘。
