#PART 1: Options from Words in Context Questions (Module 1 Q1–4 & Module 2 Q1–4)
complimented by|phr.|praised by|受到……稱讚|The chef was ______ every guest at the dinner.|主廚「受到」晚宴上每位客人的「稱讚」。|complimented by
uncertain about|phr.|not sure about|對……不確定的|I am still ______ which club to join.|我還「不確定」要參加哪個社團。|uncertain about
unbothered by|phr.|not worried or annoyed by|不為……所困擾的|The cat seemed ______ the loud music.|那隻貓似乎「不受」吵鬧音樂「影響」。|unbothered by
inspired by|phr.|given ideas or a feeling to create something by|受到……啟發|Her song was ______ her grandmother's stories.|她的歌「受到」祖母故事的「啟發」。|inspired by
uncontroversial|adj.|not causing disagreement|沒有爭議的|Adding more trees to the park was an ______ decision.|在公園增加更多樹木是一個「沒有爭議的」決定。
individualistic|adj.|independent and different from others|個人主義的；與眾不同的|The designer is known for her bold, ______ style.|這位設計師以大膽、「與眾不同」的風格聞名。
theoretical|adj.|based on ideas rather than practice|理論上的|The plan works in a ______ way, but no one has tested it yet.|這個計畫在「理論上」可行，但還沒有人試過。
homogeneous|adj.|all of the same kind; not varied|同質的；單一的|The class is not ______; students come from many countries.|這個班級並非「單一同質」；學生來自許多國家。
cause|n.|the reason why something happens|原因|Scientists are studying the ______ of the fire.|科學家正在研究火災的「原因」。
existence|n.|the fact of being real or present|存在|Few people knew of the island's ______.|很少人知道這座島的「存在」。
implication|n.|a possible effect or result of something|含意；可能的影響|The ______s of the new law are still unclear.|新法律可能帶來的「影響」仍不清楚。
scale|n.|the size or level of something|規模；程度|People were shocked by the ______ of the damage.|人們對損害的「規模」感到震驚。
exceed|v.|to be more than a certain amount|超過|Do not ______ the speed limit.|不要「超過」速限。
diminish|v.|to become or make smaller or less|減少；削弱|The pain will ______ after a few days.|疼痛幾天後會「減輕」。
encompass|v.|to include or surround|包含；涵蓋|The national park ______es mountains, lakes, and forests.|這座國家公園「涵蓋」山脈、湖泊和森林。
conceal|v.|to hide something|隱藏；遮掩|He tried to ______ his smile.|他試圖「掩飾」他的笑容。
tenuous|adj.|very weak or slight|薄弱的；微弱的|The link between the two events is ______ at best.|這兩件事之間的關聯頂多算是「薄弱的」。
enduring|adj.|lasting for a long time|持久的|Their ______ friendship began in first grade.|他們「持久的」友誼從一年級就開始了。
contentious|adj.|causing a lot of disagreement|有爭議的|School uniforms are a ______ topic among students.|校服在學生之間是個「有爭議的」話題。
conspicuous|adj.|very easy to notice|顯眼的；引人注目的|She wore a ______ red hat so her friends could find her.|她戴了一頂「顯眼的」紅帽子，好讓朋友找到她。
insensible to|phr.|not aware of or not affected by|對……無感的；未察覺的|He seemed ______ the cold wind.|他似乎「感覺不到」寒風。|insensible to
manifest in|phr.|clearly shown in|顯現於……|Her kindness is ______ the way she treats animals.|她的善良「表現在」她對待動物的方式上。|manifest in
scrutinized by|phr.|examined very carefully by|受到……仔細審查|Every page of the report was ______ the editors.|報告的每一頁都「受到」編輯的「仔細審查」。|scrutinized by
complicated by|phr.|made more difficult by|因……而複雜化|The rescue was ______ heavy snow.|救援行動「因」大雪「而變得複雜」。|complicated by
exacerbate|v.|to make a bad situation worse|使惡化；加劇|Scratching will only ______ the itch.|抓癢只會「加劇」搔癢。
redress|v.|to correct something that is wrong or unfair|矯正；彌補|The school added new courses to ______ the lack of art classes.|學校增設新課程，以「彌補」美術課的不足。
epitomize|v.|to be a perfect example of something|作為……的典型；體現|This small café ______s the town's friendly spirit.|這間小咖啡館「體現」了小鎮友善的精神。
preclude|v.|to prevent something from happening|排除；阻止|Bad weather may ______ an outdoor ceremony.|惡劣天氣可能使戶外典禮「無法舉行」。
argue about|phr.|to disagree in words about something|爭論|My brothers often ______ who gets the front seat.|我的兄弟們常「爭論」誰坐前座。|argue about
disapprove of|phr.|to think something is bad or wrong|不贊成|My parents ______ playing video games late at night.|我父母「不贊成」深夜打電動。|disapprove of
assert possession of|phr.|to claim that something belongs to you|宣稱擁有|Both countries ______ the small island.|兩國都「宣稱擁有」這座小島。|assert possession of
provide resistance to|phr.|to act against or block something|提供阻力；阻擋|Thick walls ______ strong winds.|厚牆能「抵擋」強風。|provide resistance to
#PART 2: Key Words from Module 1 Passages
misconception|n.|a wrong idea or belief|誤解；錯誤觀念|It is a common ______ that bats are blind.|蝙蝠是瞎子是一個常見的「誤解」。
notable|adj.|worth noticing; important|值得注意的；顯著的|The museum has a ______ collection of ancient coins.|這間博物館有一批「值得注意的」古錢幣收藏。
densely|adv.|closely packed together|密集地|The forest is so ______ planted that little light reaches the ground.|森林種得太「密」，很少光線能照到地面。
vegetation|n.|plants in general, especially in an area|植被；植物|Thick ______ covers the riverbanks.|濃密的「植被」覆蓋著河岸。
enthusiasm|n.|great excitement and interest|熱忱；熱情|The kids showed great ______ for the science fair.|孩子們對科學展展現了極大的「熱忱」。
hygiene|n.|practices that keep people clean and healthy|衛生|Good ______ helps prevent illness.|良好的「衛生」習慣有助於預防疾病。
amenity|n.|a useful feature or service that makes life comfortable|便利設施|The hotel's ______s include a pool and a gym.|這家飯店的「設施」包括游泳池和健身房。
spontaneously|adv.|naturally, without planning|自發地；自然地|The audience ______ began to sing along.|觀眾「自發地」開始跟著唱。
virtuosity|n.|great skill in music or another art|精湛的技藝|The violinist's ______ amazed the crowd.|那位小提琴家「精湛的技藝」讓觀眾驚嘆。
excavation|n.|the act of digging to find buried objects|挖掘；發掘|The ______ uncovered ancient coins and pots.|這次「挖掘」出土了古代錢幣和陶罐。
upend|v.|to completely change or overturn|推翻；顛覆|The discovery could ______ what we know about dinosaurs.|這項發現可能「顛覆」我們對恐龍的認識。
premature|adj.|happening too early|過早的|It is ______ to celebrate before the game is over.|比賽結束前就慶祝還「太早」。
crude|adj.|simple and roughly made|粗糙的；簡陋的|The children built a ______ raft from sticks.|孩子們用樹枝做了一個「簡陋的」木筏。
ambient|adj.|in the surrounding area|周遭的；環境的|The café plays soft ______ music.|咖啡館播放輕柔的「背景」音樂。
posit|v.|to suggest something as a fact or basis for argument|假定；提出|Scientists ______ that the planet once had oceans.|科學家「假定」這顆行星曾經有海洋。
causality|n.|the relationship between a cause and its effect|因果關係|The study shows a link but not ______.|這項研究顯示有關聯，但並非「因果關係」。
colonize|v.|to settle in and take control of a new area|殖民；在……定居|Plants quickly ______ new volcanic islands.|植物很快就在新的火山島上「落地生根」。
lineage|n.|a line of descent from ancestors|血統；演化支系|The family can trace its ______ back 500 years.|這個家族的「血統」可以追溯到 500 年前。
pluck|v.|to pull quickly with the fingers|撥（弦）；拔|He ______ed the guitar strings gently.|他輕輕「撥動」吉他弦。
toxic|adj.|poisonous|有毒的|Some mushrooms are ______ to humans.|有些蘑菇對人類「有毒」。
illuminate|v.|to light up|照亮|Lanterns ______ the path at night.|燈籠在夜裡「照亮」小路。
smother|v.|to cover something so that it cannot get air|悶住；使窒息|He ______ed the small fire with a wet blanket.|他用濕毯子「悶熄」小火。
unconventional|adj.|not following the usual way|非傳統的；不尋常的|She took an ______ path to becoming a doctor.|她走了一條「不尋常的」路成為醫生。
fragmented|adj.|broken into small pieces|破碎的；分裂的|The ______ ice floated down the river.|「碎裂的」冰塊順著河流漂下。
moniker|n.|a nickname|綽號；稱號|Chicago has the ______ "the Windy City."|芝加哥有「風城」的「稱號」。
entrepreneur|n.|a person who starts a business|企業家；創業家|The young ______ opened three cafés in two years.|這位年輕的「創業家」兩年內開了三家咖啡館。
intricately|adv.|in a very detailed and complicated way|錯綜複雜地；精細地|The box was ______ carved with flowers.|這個盒子上「精細地」雕刻著花朵。
crossbreed|v.|to mix two different breeds or varieties|雜交|Farmers ______ cows to get more milk.|農民讓牛「雜交」以獲得更多牛奶。
hybrid|n.|a mix of two different kinds|混種；混合物|A mule is a ______ of a horse and a donkey.|騾是馬和驢的「混種」。
molten|adj.|melted by heat|熔化的|______ rock flows from the volcano.|「熔化的」岩石從火山流出。
#PART 3: Key Words from Module 2 Passages
overshadow|v.|to make something seem less important|使相形失色；掩蓋|Her brother's success ______ed her own achievements.|她哥哥的成功「掩蓋」了她自己的成就。
concurrent|adj.|happening at the same time|同時發生的|The two ______ festivals drew huge crowds.|兩個「同時舉行的」節慶吸引了大批人潮。
status quo|n.|the existing state of things|現狀|Some people resist change and prefer the ______.|有些人抗拒改變，偏好「現狀」。

ornamentation|n.|decoration|裝飾|The church is famous for its golden ______.|這座教堂以金色的「裝飾」聞名。
meticulously|adv.|very carefully, with attention to detail|一絲不苟地|She ______ checked every answer.|她「一絲不苟地」檢查每一個答案。
exemplify|v.|to be a typical example of|作為……的範例|These paintings ______ the artist's early style.|這些畫作是這位藝術家早期風格的「典型範例」。
cohort|n.|a group of people with something in common|一群人；同一族群|This year's ______ of students is the largest ever.|今年這「一批」學生是歷來人數最多的。
multitude|n.|a very large number of people or things|大量；群眾|A ______ of stars filled the sky.|「無數」星星布滿天空。
festive|adj.|happy and suitable for a celebration|節慶的；歡樂的|The streets had a ______ mood during the parade.|遊行期間街道充滿「歡樂的」氣氛。
evocative|adj.|bringing strong images or memories to mind|令人聯想的；喚起回憶的|The smell of rain is ______ of my childhood.|雨的氣味「讓我想起」童年。
empowerment|n.|giving people more control over their lives|賦權；增能|Education is a key to women's ______.|教育是女性「賦權」的關鍵。
zenith|n.|the highest point|頂點；巔峰|The empire reached its ______ in the 1500s.|這個帝國在 1500 年代達到「巔峰」。
militant|adj.|aggressive and forceful in support of a cause|激進的；好戰的|The group's ______ tactics scared some supporters.|這個團體「激進的」手段嚇跑了一些支持者。
espouse|v.|to support an idea or belief|擁護；支持|She ______s the idea that every child should learn to swim.|她「支持」每個孩子都該學游泳的想法。
sustainability|n.|the ability to continue without harming the environment|永續性|The town focuses on ______ by recycling and using solar power.|這個小鎮透過回收和使用太陽能來重視「永續性」。
biodiversity|n.|the variety of living things in an area|生物多樣性|Rainforests have high ______.|熱帶雨林有很高的「生物多樣性」。
skepticism|n.|doubt about whether something is true|懷疑；懷疑態度|The claim was met with ______.|這個說法遭到「質疑」。
counterintuitive|adj.|opposite to what one would expect|違反直覺的|It seems ______, but drinking warm tea can cool you down.|這似乎「違反直覺」，但喝熱茶能讓你涼快。
destabilize|v.|to make something unstable|使不穩定|Heavy rain can ______ the hillside.|大雨可能使山坡「不穩定」。
averse|adj.|strongly disliking or opposed to|反感的；不願意的|My cat is ______ to baths.|我的貓「討厭」洗澡。
biosignature|n.|a sign that shows life exists or existed|生物標記；生命跡象|Scientists search for ______s on other planets.|科學家在其他行星上尋找「生命跡象」。
permeate|v.|to spread through something|滲透；瀰漫|The smell of bread ______d the whole house.|麵包的香味「瀰漫」整間屋子。
impede|v.|to slow down or block|妨礙；阻礙|Fallen trees ______d the rescue team.|倒下的樹木「阻礙」了救援隊。
stereotypic|adj.|repeated without purpose (of animal behavior); fixed and typical|刻板的；重複無目的的|Bored zoo animals may show ______ pacing.|無聊的動物園動物可能出現「刻板的」踱步行為。
inadvertently|adv.|without meaning to; by accident|無意中；不經意地|He ______ deleted the file.|他「不小心」刪除了檔案。
iconic|adj.|widely known and admired as a symbol|具代表性的；標誌性的|The Eiffel Tower is an ______ landmark.|艾菲爾鐵塔是「具代表性的」地標。
diffraction|n.|the bending of waves around edges|繞射|______ makes light spread out after passing through a narrow gap.|「繞射」使光穿過狹縫後散開。
preeminent|adj.|better than all others; most important|卓越的；首屈一指的|She is the ______ expert on ancient Egypt.|她是古埃及研究「首屈一指的」專家。
attribute|v.|to say that something was created or caused by someone|歸因於；認為……是某人所作|The poem is ______d to an unknown monk.|這首詩「被認為是」一位不知名僧侶所作。

hindsight|n.|understanding of an event after it has happened|事後的認識|In ______, I should have studied earlier.|「事後看來」，我應該早點念書。
paradigm|n.|a typical model or way of thinking|典範；範式|The invention of the internet created a new ______ for communication.|網路的發明創造了溝通的新「典範」。
crepuscular|adj.|active at dawn and dusk|晨昏活動的|Deer are ______ animals.|鹿是「晨昏活動的」動物。
replica|n.|an exact copy|複製品|The museum displays a ______ of the ancient ship.|博物館展示這艘古船的「複製品」。
